# Plunge Copyright and Takedown Policy

> DRAFT: not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Note for the operator: to rely on the U.S. DMCA safe harbour you generally need to register a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office, and the details here must match that registration. This has not been done yet.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

We respect the rights of game developers, publishers and other creators. Plunge profiles must not contain a game's own content (models, textures, sounds, levels, code or packaged assets), and the hub rejects files that look like it. If you believe something on the hub infringes your rights, tell us.

## How to send a notice

Send a written notice to:

Elizabeth Lennox
Dallas, Texas, United States
copyright@plungevr.com

We have not yet registered a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office. We still read every notice sent to this address and act on valid ones promptly.

Your notice should include:

- your physical or electronic signature, as the rights holder or someone authorised to act for them
- a description of the work you say is infringed
- the address (link) or name of the profile on the hub that you say infringes it, in enough detail for us to find it
- your contact details (address, phone, email)
- a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law
- a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them

Knowingly sending a false notice can make you liable for damages.

## What we do

When we get a valid notice we will promptly remove or disable access to the profile and tell the person who published it. We may also remove the profile while we look into a complaint that is not yet complete.

## Counter-notice

If your profile was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the same agent with:

- your signature
- the profile that was removed and where it appeared before removal
- a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification
- your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you agree to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, outside the US, for Dallas County, Texas) and will accept service from the person who sent the notice

If the rights holder does not tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they have started a court action, we may restore the profile.

## Repeat infringers

We will end the publishing access of people who repeatedly upload infringing content. Because every upload comes from an account, we do this by removing their content and closing their account.

## Other rights and non-copyright problems

For trademark, privacy or malware reports, or content that breaks the Community Guidelines, write to support@plungevr.com.
